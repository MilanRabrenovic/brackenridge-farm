# Laptop playtest — Brackenridge Farm

October 15, 2026. Use this after the local build/checks; human testing and Fire TV acceptance are the final gates, not prerequisites for implementation.

## Start

1. Keep `npm run dev` running. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8787/host/`.
2. Scan the current QR on a same-Wi-Fi phone. Compare the phone's ROOM badge with the host code; an old tab may control a different room.
3. Press Enter on the laptop to admit the phone. One phone starts the assisted solo weekend. For co-op, join/admit both during the arrival lesson. If arrival already ended, press R with both connected to restart as co-op.
4. Drag to walk; release to stop. ACTION interacts or holds. Tractor mode has EXIT and Engage field tool. Phone utility buttons share your clue, return an item, or pause.

## What the implemented weekend contains

| Job | Expected interaction |
|---|---|
| Arrival | Reach CHECK HAY and tap ACTION. Try entering/exiting the tractor. |
| Escaped flock | Sheep jump and scatter. Approach from below to guide them north. The gate keeper opens an eight-second window and can help herd. Solo helper holds it. |
| Team improvement | Choose rain covers or maintenance; hold together for three seconds to spend £20, or wait to save. |
| Spluttering machine | Mechanic inspects and reads the private diagnosis. Runner fetches the requested part. Mechanic pumps/releases to keep pressure green while runner holds to fit. Solo helper balances pressure. |
| Beat the rain | Tractor cultivates all marked plots, engages sowing, waits through growth, engages harvest. Storage crew seals intake, covers apples and optionally prepares crates. EXIT then ACTION stores harvested grain. |
| Buyer briefing / delivery | Share separate buyer/stock clues. Carry two correct loads from their store to packing. Inspect buyer and hold together at van. Failed earlier work still permits a recovery delivery. |
| Farm report | Review outcomes/contributions/balance. Ready on both phones starts a new seed; tap again cancels a waiting vote. Solo requires one tap. |

Task cards and compass arrows show public destinations/actions. Arrows indicate direction, not a route through walls. The minimap helps choose roads/gates. The correct repair part and buyer goods still come from phone clues.

## Record, without coaching the first run

- Does each player know their immediate job and destination? Record wrong turns and requests for help.
- Does both-player communication matter? Record idle periods, confusing clues and whether one player does everything.
- Are sheep evasion and the pressure game playful or frustrating? Do crop tool changes and storage holds feel clear?
- Can both players read the split view, task cards, timer and phone text comfortably?
- Does anyone walk through a solid object, get trapped, see a broken animation, or lose their controller mode?
- Does a missed deadline clearly explain recovery? Can players finish and choose replay?
- Briefly disconnect/reconnect a phone: the game should pause, preserve the job and resume appropriately. A disconnected player must re-confirm a waiting replay vote.

The deadline budget is about 14–15 minutes, not a measured normal completion time. Automated routes use accelerated simulation and do not establish fun. Keep a list of exact failures and their room/seed/phase rather than reporting only that the game feels broken.

## After the laptop run

Fix observed defects, then repeat the successful/failure/reconnect/replay route on Fire TV, checking remote/QR/phone input, graphics, sound, sustained performance and readability. Record qualifying footage only from a verified route. Source/video/Devpost publication remains a separate final task.
