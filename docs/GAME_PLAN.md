# Hackathon game plan — implemented slice

Updated 2026-09-30. The active committed implementation and rules are in `../GAME_DIRECTION.md`; prior expansion plans must not override the user's later 2D/gameplay decisions.

## Current slice

Two-player original 2D Brackenridge Farm, one connected map, phone-private clues, adaptive close split cameras. Arrival, animal guide-and-gate, equipment repair/supply, weather/crop protection, market dispatch, one confirmed planning purchase and a delivery briefing and results/replay are connected. Seeded bounded timing, belt/gasket repair faults and buyer-order variants supply replay variation. Solo is a full assisted test weekend with normal deadlines and the entire crop lifecycle. Four players and Bedrock are deferred.

## Validation gate

Automated interaction/collision/relay/export tests plus native/browser inspection are complete for the September 30 candidate. Human cooperative pacing, final art acceptance and qualifying Fire TV performance/remote/demo are still required. Read `../PROJECT_STATE.md` for exact results and `SUBMISSION.md` for the remaining submission steps. Never equate a signed APK or source-tree test with successful Fire TV execution.

## Expansion boundary

No cattle, dairy, shop/brewery, construction, vehicle physics, additional map, playable combine, implements, extra farmers or authored work animation sheets in the current slice. Improve these only after testing the existing complete weekend on its intended device.
